# Response to Claire Chen's Evaluating Design Blog

I like that you addressed one of the key limitations of cognitive walkthroughs. Placing yourself in another person's shoes can be challenging, so it is important to be aware of the target demographic. I have provided some feedback and insights to your blog below.

## Feedback

- Good job on mentioning the main challenge of identifying age biases in app creation and making your app accessible to different age groups.

- Though it is not required, I would like to see examples of age biases that you identified in _your_ current design. For example, does your app assume that the user is comfortable with visually complex interfaces? What else comes easy to you about the app that may not come as easily to older users?

- I would also like to see further detail on why your navigation process is not as understandable to elderly users, as well as temporary solutions that came to mind.

## New Ideas and Insights

- If your goal is to provide good user experiences for older audiences, I encourage you to look general-purpose apps that have done this successfully and make a note of the features that make this possible. For example, a lot of older members of my family use WhatsApp, and I imagine that it is due to its option to send voice recordings. Older audiences prefer vocal conversation, so having this feature in addition to the youth-friendly text feature is a great way to cater to different age demographics.

- On the other hand, what are apps that cater really well to younger users but disregard older users. Is there a way to retain these types of features while providing ample avenues for older users?
